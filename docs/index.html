How the numbers were counted

Nothing here is typed in by hand. CauseTable computes the groups from the export at render time: uniqueById first (the export repeats rows across pages — 427 rows are 412 reports), then causeOf for each report, from its channel and its tenant’s booking form. The 5 reports the desk read as a one-day move stay where they are filed, under parser, and are footnoted under the table.
